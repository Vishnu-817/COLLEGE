What makes Summit College different?+

Small classes, accessible faculty, and a close-knit campus make it easier to find support and get involved. You’ll learn across disciplines, apply what you know, and build a path that feels like your own.

How do I apply for financial aid?+

Once you apply for admission, our financial aid team can guide you through the FAFSA and Summit scholarship opportunities. About 85% of our students receive some form of financial aid.

Can I visit campus before applying?+

Absolutely. Reach out through our contact form and our admissions team will help you plan a campus visit, meet students, and sit in on a class when available.

When should I apply for Fall 2027?+

Applications are open now. Request information above to connect with an admissions counselor who can walk you through deadlines and the application process.
